The Canlander Council announced its Fall 2026 changes on August 31, effective **September 1, 2026**. The theme is combo: fast storm decks, Time Vault, and Flash Hulk had been winning more than the council liked, and every change targets the cards that help them assemble their pieces.

<div class="change-table">

| Card | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enlightened Tutor | 0 | **1** |
| Spellseeker | 0 | **1** |
| The Fantasticar | 0 | **1** |
| Tolarian Academy | 1 | **2** |

</div>

Two other proposals were voted on and not adopted: Demonic Counsel and Yawgmoth's Will both stay at zero for now.

## What it means if you're new

If you're building your first deck, the practical upshot is small. These are mostly cards for combo specialists. If you copied a recent Academy or Vault list, though, recount your points. A list that was exactly ten in August is probably over now.

The next update is scheduled for **December 1, 2026**, announced the day before.
