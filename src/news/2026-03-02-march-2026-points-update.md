The council's March update responded to how the format had shifted since its change in philosophy the year before. Control and combo decks were on top, and two cards were singled out for propping them up.

<div class="change-table">

| Card | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The One Ring | 0 | **1** |
| Tamiyo, Inquisitive Student | 0 | **1** |
| Time Walk | 6 | **5** |
| Thassa's Oracle | 6 | **5** |
| Minsc & Boo, Timeless Heroes | 1 | **0** |

</div>

Proposals to drop Wrenn and Six to zero and raise Tolarian Academy to two were considered and didn't get enough votes. (Academy went to two in the fall anyway.)

## Why lower Time Walk?

Lowering points is how the council makes room for decks that were being squeezed out. At five, Time Walk fits alongside a Mox or a pair of tutors, which opens up more combinations than six did.
