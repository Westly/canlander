Canadian Highlander has a passionate community, an official home at [canadianhighlander.ca](https://canadianhighlander.ca/), and a busy Discord. What it hasn't had is one place to send a curious friend who has never heard of it.

That's what this site is for. Three things, mainly:

- **A gentle introduction.** The [Learn](/learn/) page explains the rules and the points list in plain language, with advice for building and playing a first deck.
- **A tidy list of links.** [Resources](/resources/) collects the official sources, community spaces, results archives, and tools in one spot.
- **Short news posts.** When the points list changes, we'll summarize what moved and link to the council's own reasoning.

We're not the official source and we don't want to be. When the council publishes something, we point to it.

If you know a resource we should add, or spot something wrong, the site's source is on GitHub and suggestions are welcome.
